# Authorship, Ownership & Licensing

## The database

The **radiometric emissometers database** was conceived jointly by
**Jon Gabirondo-López** and **Josu M. Igartua**, its intellectual authors:
they designed it, articulated the case for its existence, and are committed to
maintaining, extending, and updating it — including the planned incorporation
of the measurement data produced by the instruments it catalogs.

- **Jon Gabirondo-López** — compilation of the literature corpus: gathering,
  sorting, and manually inspecting every article; extraction and normalization
  of the records.
- **Josu M. Igartua** — creator of **Emissary**, the application.

The dataset is published as
[radiometric-emissometers-db](https://github.com/jongablop/radiometric-emissometers-db)
(Zenodo DOI [10.5281/zenodo.21762418](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21762418));
please cite it using its own `CITATION.cff`.

## Ownership

The compilation of the information, and the Emissary application, belong to the
**Thermophysical Properties of Materials Research Group** of the University of
the Basque Country (UPV/EHU) — the group that also owns and operates the
**HAIRL** emissometer in Leioa
(Del Campo et al., *Rev. Sci. Instrum.* 2006, doi:10.1063/1.2393157;
updated uncertainty budget in *Metrologia* 2020, doi:10.1088/1681-7575/ab84ff).
