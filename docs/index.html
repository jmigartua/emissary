Emissary · a macOS app for the radiometric emissometers database

The instrument database, as one view per instrument

Download for macOS ↓ v0.6.1 · Intel (Apple-silicon build planned) · free, open data (CC-BY-4.0)

Everything below is the app itself, running on the real dataset in your browser — all 139 records of radiometric-emissometers-db (Gabirondo-López, Arredondo & Igartua, Zenodo · CC-BY-4.0). Three commitments shape the design: the window chrome stays monochrome so data is the only color; measurement geometry is the one categorical encoding (colorblind-validated); and every instrument gets a fingerprint — a single plot that states its capability completely.

2 · The mandatory plot — Capability plane

The whole field in one view: every instrument is a rectangle spanning its wavelength range (x, log) and temperature range (y). Color is measurement geometry. The empty regions are the research gaps — hemispherical coverage, sub-ambient temperatures, far-IR. Hover any mark; click to open it in the inspector above; toggle classes in the legend.

1Nothing is silently dropped. 78 records have both ranges and appear as rectangles; records with only a λ-span or only a T-span are drawn as edge strips along the matching axis; the caption counts every group.

3 · The instrument fingerprint

Your requirement — "all the information in just a view" — becomes a component. The fingerprint renders any record as one compact plot: both capability spans set against the corpus's coverage density, geometry as a glyph, methods as chips. It appears at three sizes: micro (list rows), standard (inspector), and full (below, annotated).

Fingerprint —
Open record
1Corpus context is the gray shadow. Behind each colored span, a density strip shows how many of the 139 instruments cover each wavelength / temperature — so "wide" or "hot" is read relative to the field, instantly.
2Geometry glyph, not a word. A single arrow (normal), a fan (directional), or a dome (hemispherical) — legible at 12 px, identical everywhere in the app.
3Uncertain is visible. Spans whose source string carried the curator's "?" render with a dashed end-cap rather than a clean edge — the raw string is always one hover away.

Download

Emissary is a native macOS app (Tauri 2, ~12 MB) that ships with the full dataset bundled and can point at any live checkout of radiometric-emissometers-db. Get the DMG from github.com/jmigartua/emissary/releases (unsigned build: right-click → Open on first launch), or build from source:

git clone https://github.com/jmigartua/emissary && cd emissary
npx @tauri-apps/cli build        # → src-tauri/target/release/bundle/dmg/Emissary_0.1.0_*.dmg

Authorship & ownership

The database was conceived jointly by Jon Gabirondo-López and Josu M. Igartua — its intellectual authors and maintainers, who will keep extending it, with the measurement data produced by these instruments planned as the next layer. Jon Gabirondo-López compiled, sorted and manually inspected the article corpus. Josu M. Igartua created the Emissary application. The compilation and the app belong to the Thermophysical Properties of Materials Research Group of the University of the Basque Country (UPV/EHU) — the group that also owns and operates the HAIRL emissometer in Leioa.
