Emissary manual

Emissary is a reader and curation instrument for the radiometric-emissometers-db dataset — 139 published emissometer designs, 1917–2026. The dataset's JSON files remain the source of truth; the app ships with a bundled copy and can point at any live checkout via Open dataset… (pick the repo folder, its data/ folder, or data/papers/).

Views

  • Library — every record, searchable and faceted. Each row previews the instrument's spectral span (top bar, log 0.2–1000 µm) and temperature span (bottom bar, 0–3100 K) against the full corpus width. The dot color is measurement geometry; a dashed ring means the paper doesn't state it. Click a row to open it in the inspector.
  • Inspector (right panel) — the record, whole: the fingerprint plot (both capability spans over the corpus-coverage shadow in gray), bibliography, citation total with a freshness tag (stale means the Scopus count is older than the record's last edit), raw method strings (dashed chips, exactly as published) beside the normalized family (solid chip), in-corpus cites / cited-by as clickable chips, and the append-only changelog.
  • Capability plane — the whole field in one plot: wavelength × temperature rectangles, colored by geometry. Records missing one range appear as strips along the matching axis; the top-right caption counts every group. Click a rectangle to inspect it.
  • Timeline — one column per year, stacked by geometry. Hover for the year's records; click to open the first.
  • Atlas — records per corresponding country (ISO codes, from the dataset's location block).
  • Health — live data-quality tiles computed from the loaded dataset. Amber and red tiles are clickable and open the offending records as a filtered Library view (dismiss the filter with the ✕ chip in the sidebar).
  • Vocabulary — all raw detection / temperature-method strings grouped by collapsed key, so "electic heater" sits beside "electric heater". This is the normalization queue for Phase 2.

Keyboard

  • ⌘K — command palette: type to jump to any record or view; ↑↓ to move, ⏎ to open, Esc to close.
  • ⌘1…⌘6 — switch views.
  • Esc — close the palette.

Filters

Geometry and family chips are additive toggles — pressed chips are included. The search box matches title, authors, venue, methods and country. Sorting: year, citations, or author. Filters never recolor marks: color always follows the geometry class.
