Emissary manual

Emissary is a reader and curation instrument for the radiometric-emissometers-db dataset — 139 published emissometer designs, 1917–2026. The dataset's JSON files remain the source of truth; the app ships with a bundled copy and can point at any live checkout via Open dataset… (pick the repo folder, its data/ folder, or data/papers/).

Views

  • Library — every record, searchable and faceted. Each row previews the instrument's spectral span (top bar, log 0.2–1000 µm) and temperature span (bottom bar, 0–3100 K) against the full corpus width. The dot color is measurement geometry; a dashed ring means the paper doesn't state it. Click a row to open it in the inspector.
  • Inspector (right panel) — the record, whole: the fingerprint plot (both capability spans over the corpus-coverage shadow in gray), bibliography, citation total with a freshness tag (stale means the Scopus count is older than the record's last edit), raw method strings (dashed chips, exactly as published) beside the normalized family (solid chip), in-corpus cites / cited-by as clickable chips, and the append-only changelog.
  • Capability plane — the whole field in one plot: wavelength × temperature rectangles, colored by geometry. Records missing one range appear as strips along the matching axis; the top-right caption counts every group. Click a rectangle to inspect it.
  • Timeline — one column per year, stacked by geometry. Hover for the year's records; click to open the first.
  • Atlas — a world map: countries shaded by records per corresponding country (√ scale so China doesn't crush Europe), instrument locations as dots at affiliation cities (hover for the instruments, click to open them), and count bars below. Scroll to zoom toward the cursor, drag to pan, double-click or ⤢ to reset.
  • Health — live data-quality tiles computed from the loaded dataset, including the distinct-instrument estimate (records that look like updates of an earlier same-city instrument). Amber and red tiles are clickable and open the offending records as a filtered Library view (dismiss the filter with the ✕ chip in the sidebar).
  • Vocabulary — all raw detection / temperature-method strings grouped by collapsed key, so "electic heater" sits beside "electric heater". This is the normalization queue for Phase 2.

Keyboard

  • ⌘K — command palette: type to jump to any record or view; ↑↓ to move, ⏎ to open, Esc to close.
  • ⌘1…⌘6 — switch views; ⌘, — Settings; ⌘[ / ⌘] — back / forward through your navigation history.
  • Esc — close the palette.

Filters

Geometry and family chips are additive toggles — pressed chips are included. The search box matches title, authors, venue, methods and country. Sorting: year, citations, or author. Filters never recolor marks: color always follows the geometry class.

About — authorship & ownership

The database was conceived jointly by Jon Gabirondo-López and Josu M. Igartua, its intellectual authors, who maintain and extend it — with the measurement data produced by these instruments planned as its next layer. Jon Gabirondo-López compiled, sorted and manually inspected the article corpus; Josu M. Igartua created this application. The compilation and the app belong to the Thermophysical Properties of Materials Research Group (UPV/EHU) — also owner of the HAIRL emissometer in Leioa. Free to use: dataset CC-BY-4.0, app MIT.

Release notes

0.6.0 — Precision atlas: Natural Earth 10m geometry (Douglas-Peucker-simplified to ~1 km tolerance) with zoom to 120× — coastlines stay true down to city scale. City labels now cull collisions (largest first) and flip at map edges, ending the stray-label problem. Country bars are interactive: hover for the full record list (↩ marks updates of an earlier record), click to open those records in the Library (dismissable country chip; back/forward aware).

0.5.0 — Counts now mean instruments: the Atlas, its bars and its location dots collapse lineage-linked records (Spain: 5 records → 4 instruments, the HAIRL counted once; China: 53 → 48), and tooltips state all three tiers — instruments · records · research groups (same city + shared authors; China has only 23, Xinxiang's 18 records are one group). High-resolution map (Natural Earth 50m): coastlines and borders stay true at 40× zoom, so locations are genuinely visible. Sidebar icons restored, with a Settings choice of icons · labels · both.

0.4.0 — Records vs instruments made explicit: the dataset counts publications (new instruments or substantial modifications), so the app now labels map counts as records, adds an Instrument lineage section to the inspector (a record citing an earlier in-corpus record from the same affiliation city is flagged as a likely update — e.g. the HAIRL at UPV/EHU Leioa appears as its 2006 instrument paper plus its 2020 uncertainty-budget update), and a Health tile estimating distinct instruments (≤128 of 139). Country tooltips now list the records. Map zoom extended to 40× with city · count labels appearing beyond 5×. BibTeX escapes in author names ("Ballestr{\'\i}n") now render as proper unicode.

0.3.0 — Atlas upgrades: visible country outlines, scroll-to-zoom and drag-to-pan (buttons top-right, double-click resets), and the 66 instrument locations plotted as dots at affiliation cities — hover for the instruments there, click to open them. Navigation history: ‹ › buttons and ⌘[ / ⌘] walk back and forward through the places you've been (view, record, filter). Search now also matches affiliation cities.

0.2.0 — Atlas world map (choropleth of records per corresponding country, √ scale; click a country to open its records). Technical tooltips: IR band names (NIR/SWIR/MWIR/LWIR/FIR), Wien-displacement peak with in-band check (λ = 2898 µm·K / T), optional wavenumbers (cm⁻¹), methods and temperature metrology on hover. Settings view (⌘,): theme override, Kelvin/Celsius, wavenumbers, Wien check, tooltip verbosity, fingerprint shadow, view on launch — stored locally, applied immediately.

0.1.0 — first release. Read-only reader over the canonical dataset: Library + inspector with instrument fingerprint, capability plane, timeline, atlas, health dashboard, vocabulary queue, ⌘K palette, bundled dataset with live-checkout override, DOI opening. Editing, git integration and vocabulary mapping land in Phase 2; FER-format measurement spectra (SiC, EKHI) in Phase 3.
